**Overfitting risk.** F5–F8 were fixed while I was looking at the main evaluation questions. To measure that bias, I wrote ten **new** questions (`eval/holdout.json`) after the fixes and looked up their expected answers in the documents. I then ran them once without changing the system. Result: 10/10 passed, with 16/16 citations valid. That set includes F3 and F4, which show that a pass on fact checks can still hide a quality issue.

## 2. Documentation findings and recommended improvements

The ingest checks found items 1–4 automatically and send them to the expert review queue (`python -m docintel review`). The rest were found while reading and verifying the sources.

1. **Band lists disagree between the hardware manual and the product page.** Hardware Manual 5.3 lists **B13 and B38 for W.W** and **B38 for E.A.J** (pp. 12, 14, 54), and **B13 for N.A**. The product page's Technical Data lists none of these. Both sources give bands per *regional variant*, not per SKU. So the WW Band 38 difference is a real conflict at the same level of scope, not an SKU mismatch.
   *Improvement:* keep one band table per orderable SKU, use it for both the manual and the website, and show its revision and date on the product page.
2. **The hardware manual disagrees with itself on the L.A band list.** Band 28 appears on p. 14 (Table 2.1) but not in the lists on p. 12 (§2.1) and p. 54 (§4.1).
   *Improvement:* generate every band list from a single table.
3. **Power-consumption gaps.** Table 5.5 (pp. 60–61) has no rows for B13 and B38, even though both are listed bands, and Band 7 is "TBD".
   *Improvement:* complete the table or mark those bands as uncharacterised.
4. **A broken cross-reference.** HW §3.4.2 (p. 31) says to restart with `AT+TRB` and refers readers to the AT Command Set Manual. AT Command Manual 3.1 has no `AT+TRB`. It documents `AT$QCRST`, "This command restarts the chip" (§16.31, p. 193), and the reset parameter of `AT+CFUN` (§4.1, p. 29).
   *Improvement:* correct the command name, and cite the target document's revision and section in every cross-reference.
5. **The FCC antenna wording contradicts the module design.** §9.4.3 (p. 75) says the module "shall be only used with the internal on-board antenna… External antennas are not supported." The module, however, exposes MAIN and GNSS antenna interfaces (Table 2.1, p. 14), and §9.4.8.6 (p. 78) says "The EUT needs an external Antenna". The end-product label in §9.4.6 (p. 76) still reads "Contains FCC ID : xxxxxxxxxx".
   *Improvement:* replace the boilerplate with C16QS-specific grant conditions, the approved antenna types and gain limits, and the real FCC ID.
6. **The EVK manual is undated against the module manual.** EVK 1.1 (7 March 2023) says it is "based on" the "Cavli C16QS Hardware Manual" without giving a revision, and it predates HW 5.3 by 21 months. JMP 3 is described by position ("first position… USB Type-C") but tabulated by pin number (p. 11: pin 1 5V_ADAP, pin 3 5V_USB). USB ports are called "2x AT Port and 1x Logging" on p. 13 but "2xCOM for AT Port, 1xCOM for DM Port" on p. 20.
   *Improvement:* cite the hardware manual's revision, name jumper positions by pin, and use the same port names throughout.
7. **The product page is inconsistent with itself and with the manual:**
   - The variant-code legend says `SOON`/`SOOH`, while the table says `S00N`/`S00H`.
   - The region legend gives "EA – East Asia", the introduction says EAJ means "Eurasia and Japan", and the manual defines E.A.J as "Europe Asia & Japan" and E.A as "European Asia".
   - The introduction's region list leaves out IN and EU.
   - The sample-ordering table omits C16QS-WW-GNAH, the SKU named in the TME-hosted manual's filename, and all LA SKUs that HW Table 7.1 lists.
   - The manual's `GNBN`/`GNBH` codes are explained nowhere.

   *Improvement:* generate both the legend and the table from the HW ordering table.
8. **Revision metadata and hosting.** The product page shows no revision or date. The only public copies of the manuals I found are on third-party sites: TME, MC Technologies and itbrainpower. TME sometimes shows a browser check to scripted clients.
   *Improvement:* offer an official Cavli download centre with versioned URLs, release dates and SHA-256 checksums.
9. **Plain-language terms are missing.** Engineers ask for "size", "thickness", "upload speed" or "lowest current". The manuals use "Physical Characteristics", "UL", and mode names such as "Shutdown" and "Hibernate". (Found by the paraphrase sets, F13.)
   *Improvement:* add a glossary or alternative names to the spec tables, for example "Dimensions (size): 26.5 × 22.5 × 2.3 mm" and "Uplink (upload) peak: 5 Mbps". This helps human readers and search alike.
10. **Topics the sources never cover.** Battery-life guidance, carrier certifications (Verizon, PTCRB), MTBF and pricing all came back "not verified".
   *Improvement:* add an application note on power budgets per duty cycle and a certification matrix. Until then, the assistant should keep sending these questions to support.
11. **The PDFs are hard for machines to read.** They are Word exports with no tags, and their table cells extract out of order (F9).
    *Improvement:* publish tagged PDFs, plus the key spec tables (bands, power, pins, ordering codes) as CSV or JSON.
